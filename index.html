PocketMaps

Fold a pocket-sized map booklet from any feature service or GeoJSON.

2. Booklet

Use from ArcGIS Hub

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

PocketMap

  1. Open a dataset on any Hub site, for example its Explore view.
  2. Apply filters in Hub if you want a subset.
  3. Click the PocketMap bookmark. This app opens with the same layer and filters.

It also works on a feature layer or GeoJSON URL. Only the page address is sent, so filters must be in the URL, which Hub does automatically.

How to fold
  1. Print one side, portrait, at 100% (no "fit to page").
  2. Fold the sheet in half the long way, then unfold.
  3. Fold in half the short way, then each half in half again, so you have eight panels.
  4. Cut along the solid line in the middle (between the centre panels).
  5. Fold the long way again, push the ends together so the slit opens, and flatten into a booklet with the title page on the front.
  6. With several sheets, nest sheet 2 inside sheet 1's centre spread, and so on.
