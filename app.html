Use from ArcGIS Hub

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

PocketMap

  1. Open a dataset on any Hub site, for example its Explore view.
  2. Apply filters in Hub if you want a subset.
  3. Click the PocketMap bookmark. This app opens with the same layer and filters.

It also works on a feature layer or GeoJSON URL. Only the page address is sent, so filters must be in the URL, which Hub does automatically.

How to fold
  1. Print one side, portrait, at 100% scale.
  2. Turn it sideways, fold into eight panels, cut the middle slit, and fold into a booklet.
