Six folds and one cut

How to fold a PocketMap

Every sheet becomes an eight-page booklet. It takes about a minute, and you only need scissors.

Print at 100%Turn off “fit to page” so the panels line up with the folds.
One side onlyEach sheet is single sided. Plain letter or A4 paper works.
Use the guidesThe dashed lines on the print are the folds. The solid line in the middle is the cut.

    Which pages go on which sheet

    Sheets nest like a stapled booklet. The outer sheet carries the first and last pages.
